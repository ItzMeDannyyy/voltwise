Questions about VoltWise hardware and setup

Wiring instructions, relay ratings, and local network requirements.

ESP32 board compatibility

Any ESP32-WROOM-32 or ESP32-S3 module with at least 4MB flash supports the firmware. Connect the PZEM-004T to UART2 (GPIO 16 RX, GPIO 17 TX) and the relay trigger pins to GPIO 25 and 26.

Relay 3-second staggered restore

When power is restored after a safety cutoff or outage, the ESP32 activates Relay 1 immediately, then pauses for exactly 3.0 seconds before engaging Relay 2. This prevents motor inrush spikes from tripping your utility breaker.

ESP32 OTA firmware updates

Firmware binaries compile directly through PlatformIO and can be uploaded over your local network using ArduinoOTA or flashed over USB Type-C using standard esptool protocols.

Local MQTT broker privacy

No external cloud required. VoltWise communicates directly with an MQTT broker running on your local network (such as Mosquitto or Home Assistant). Telemetry stays inside your home network.

ESP32 offline relay protection

If Wi-Fi disconnects, the ESP32 continues autonomous protection. The PZEM-004T sensor and hardware relays operate independently of network connectivity, cutting power within 2.0s if load exceeds 3,000 W.

Relay power ceiling configuration

Yes. The Android app includes a settings panel where you can tune the trip limit between 1,000 W and 7,000 W, depending on your electrical service breaker and wire gauge ratings.
